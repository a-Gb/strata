# Code of conduct

Strata contributors are expected to make technical collaboration safe,
constructive, and accessible.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led in reviews and discussions.
- Welcome questions and explain project constraints without condescension.
- Separate critique of an idea or patch from judgment of its author.
- Respect privacy, licensing, embargoes, and the sensitivity of binary samples.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and release readiness.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
disclosure of private information, repeated disruption, and publishing
sensitive binaries or vulnerability details without consent are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when behavior threatens people, project safety, or productive work.
Use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/a-Gb/strata/security/advisories/new)
for sensitive security reports. For conduct reports, use GitHub's private
moderation/reporting controls or request private maintainer contact without
including sensitive details in public.

Good-faith reports will be handled as privately and promptly as the available
project infrastructure permits.
